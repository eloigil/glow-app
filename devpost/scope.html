GlowApp

A makeup tutor that reads your skin today and walks you, step by step, through a look rendered on your own face.

The kernel

Not “here's you, here's the end result.” It's this is you, today; these are the products you need; this is how to apply them, one layer at a time on your own photo, tailored to your skin's current state. The whole look, no need to learn makeup.

The core loop

She comes back because her skin changes day to day. Saved looks replay without spending more credits.

What a step looks like

Illustration only. Each tutorial step shows her photo with only the layers applied so far, plus an LLM-written technique tip. Real steps depend on the chosen look; the red dot stands for a concern the skin read found today.

Who it's for

The person

Women who want good makeup adapted to their skin, without spending time learning technique. For pimple and skin-condition days, and for everyday use.

What they do today

Guess, or follow generic tutorials made for someone else's face.

What “working” looks like

Demo with a face photo: upload → a couple of concerns highlighted on her face → personalized looks designed for her → pick one → all its products shown → tutorial, step by step, each step her own face with that layer applied plus technique text → save as a preferred look.

The “oh, that's cool” beat: the tutorial building up layer by layer on her face, adapted to today's skin.

Boundary

Now In the proof of concept
  • Face photo upload
  • YouCam Skin AI analysis, concerns highlighted on the photo
  • Five looks for today (skin tone + current concerns), labeled easy / medium / difficult (at least one of each, plus two more)
  • Product list per look, with plain product links (no affiliate)
  • Step-by-step tutorial: per-step render of cumulative layers + LLM technique explanation
  • Save a look as a favorite, replayable without more API credits
Later Worth doing, not now
  • “Use what I own”: list your products, some proposals use them (e.g. 2 of 5). Shape the data model now so it's a small change later.
  • Brush animation on each step (standalone, easy to add)
  • Affiliate links (Amazon referrals, etc.)
Cut Explicitly out
  • Nothing cut outright.

Constraints

~3 days to build 1,000 free YouCam API units Claude spend well under €1,000 Budget renders per step (e.g. only the chosen look) Open for spec: preview render for all 5 looks? Demo photo: one you have rights to
